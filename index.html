Privacy Policy

My Life Radar

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how My Life Radar handles information when you use the app. The app is designed to keep your journal and personal metrics on your device. The current version does not send this information to the developer or operate an account or analytics service.

Information you enter

The app stores information that you choose to enter, which may include your display name, biography, language preferences, journal titles and text, tags, dates, mood ratings, and life metrics such as sleep duration, energy, focus, exercise, social time, and learning time. The app may also store app preferences and notification settings.

How information is used

Your entries and metrics are used on your device to provide journaling, charts, summaries, search, and other app features. Your preferences are used to configure the app. If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. The app does not use your journal or metrics for advertising, profiling, or AI processing.

Where information is stored

Journal, profile, and metric data are stored in the app’s local database on your device. The database is encrypted using SQLCipher, with its key held in platform secure storage. The app has no account service or developer-operated server for this data. Device operating-system backups may include app data according to your device backup settings.

Sharing and disclosure

The app does not transmit your entries, profile, or metrics to the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties. The operating system handles local notification scheduling and biometric or device-credential checks. The app receives the authentication result; it does not receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

If you choose to export a backup, the app creates a JSON file containing your entries, metrics, and profile information at a location you select. This export is not encrypted by the app. If you save it to or share it with a cloud-storage provider, another app, or another person, that recipient will handle the file under its own terms and privacy practices. Protect exported files accordingly.

Retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app’s data. You can delete entries or use the app’s data-clearing controls. You can also remove the app and its local data through your device settings. Exported files are separate copies; delete them from every location where you saved or shared them. Device backups may retain copies under your operating-system backup settings.

Permissions

Notification permission is requested only if you choose to enable notifications. Biometric authentication is optional and controlled through the app’s lock setting and your device’s authentication system. The current Android manifest does not request internet access, contacts, camera, or location permissions.

Children

My Life Radar is not directed to children under 13, and the app is intended for general personal journaling. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through an online service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy-policy URL and will show its revision date.
